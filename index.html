About Rodd

Built on Loss. Powered by Truth.

Rodd Duff is the host and creator of Insights & Straight Talk, a podcast where raw truth meets bold voices. His journey began with the collapse of a once-successful machinery business a turning point that pushed him to rebuild his life on honesty, resilience, and purpose. What started as a personal mission has grown into a platform that inspires thousands every week.

Entrepreneur

Turning Setbacks into New Beginnings

Rodd Duff’s journey as an entrepreneur hasn’t been a straight path. He once ran a successful machinery company—until it collapsed. Instead of letting failure define him, Rodd used the experience as fuel to rebuild his life with resilience, truth, and purpose.

Today, he shares those lessons with others, proving that entrepreneurship isn’t just about profit—it’s about vision, grit, and the courage to start again. His story reminds business leaders and dreamers alike that every ending can be the start of something greater.
